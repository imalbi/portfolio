001

RAG Pipeline

ROLE  FULL-STACK, SOLE AUTHOR PERIOD  2025 SECTOR  B2B PLATFORM

Brief

The platform held a growing corpus of client documents that its users had to read manually to answer routine questions. The brief was to let them ask in natural language and get an answer grounded in those documents, with the source visible.

Constraints

  • The corpus was small at launch and expected to grow unevenly, so retrieval quality could not depend on volume.
  • PostgreSQL was already in the stack. Introducing a dedicated vector database was not justified.
  • Answers had to start appearing immediately. A blocking request that returned after several seconds was rejected in review.
  • Sole author, alongside two other client deliverables.

Architecture

FIG. 1 / RETRIEVAL PATH IN RED

Decisions

DecisionRejectedReasoning
pgvector in the existing database A dedicated vector store One database to operate, back up and join against. At this corpus size a separate service would have added infrastructure without adding recall.
HNSW index IVFFlat IVFFlat needs a representative sample to build its lists and degrades when the corpus grows unevenly. HNSW holds recall without a rebuild step.
Server-sent events WebSockets The stream is one directional. SSE reconnects on its own, survives proxies, and costs no connection state on the server.
Function calling with schema-validated arguments Parsing intent out of the model's prose Arguments arrive typed and are validated before anything executes. Prose parsing fails silently and fails often.
